2. What the Website Does (Feature → Real Benefit)

📱 Full Menu Online — 50 Items with Photos + Malayalam Names

Benefit: Customers browse your entire menu before they call. They see real photos of your laddu, halwa, cakes, samosas — and they know the price. Half your phone calls disappear because people already know what you sell. The ones who do call are ready to order.

📞 One-Tap "Order on WhatsApp" on Every Item

Benefit: Every single menu card has a button that opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message: "Hi Baby Bakers, I'd like to order Ghee Laddu (₹18/pc)." Customer taps once. That's it. No typing, no app to install (WhatsApp is already on every Kerala phone). This is the cheapest, fastest-converting ordering channel in the business.

🎨 Custom Cakes Section — with a Photo Gallery

Benefit: Birthday cakes, wedding cakes, themed cakes — these are your highest-margin items (₹500–₹2,000+ per cake). The website shows a photo collage of your past custom cakes and a big golden "Start a Custom Order" button that opens a WhatsApp chat pre-filled with the right questions (occasion, flavour, date). A single wedding-cake booking pays for the entire website.

📍 6-Location Map with Directions

Benefit: All 4 bakery outlets + 2 hypermarket stockist points on an interactive Google map. Customer taps "Directions" and gets a walking/driving route straight to the nearest shop. Nobody gets lost, nobody gives up. This alone converts a "maybe" search into a walk-in customer.

📜 "Since 1976" Story + Timeline

Benefit: When a customer sees you've been baking for 50 years — through 1976 to today — they trust you immediately. They're not buying from "a bakery," they're buying from a legacy. Trust means they don't haggle on price and they come back. This also makes you the obvious choice for festival bulk orders: "If they've been around 50 years, they're reliable."

📊 Animated Stats (50 Years / 4 Outlets / 150+ Items / Made Fresh Daily)

Benefit: Numbers build confidence faster than paragraphs. A visitor sees those four numbers count up on screen — they don't need to read anything else. They already feel they're dealing with a serious, established operation.

📱 Works on Any Phone — Perfectly

Benefit: The website is mobile-first. It looks and works exactly as well on a ₹7,000 Android phone as on a laptop. Given that 85%+ of your customers will find you on mobile, this is not optional — and this site is built for it.

✏️ You Can Edit It Yourself — No Developer Needed

Benefit: All menu items, prices, phone numbers, and location details live in one simple file (js/data.js). Open it in any text editor, change a price from "₹18" to "₹20," save, done. No coding knowledge needed. You are never held hostage by a developer for ₹500 text changes.

☘️ Runs on Free Hosting — Zero Monthly Cost

Benefit: Hosted on GitHub Pages (free, permanent). No monthly hosting bill. No server to maintain. No "your site went down because someone forgot to renew." Once it's live, it stays live.

📍 More Foot Traffic
