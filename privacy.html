Baby Bakers (“we,” “our,” or “us”) operates the website at aanandab.github.io/babybakers (the “Site”). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit our Site. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect any personal data directly through this website. Specifically:

  • We do not have user accounts, login systems, or registration forms.
  • We do not process payments on this Site.
  • We do not operate a contact form that stores your data on a server.
  • We do not use analytics tools (like Google Analytics), tracking pixels, or cookies that we set ourselves.
  • We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, or any personal identifiers through the Site.

2. WhatsApp Ordering

When you tap an “Order on WhatsApp” button on our menu, you are redirected to WhatsApp (owned by Meta Platforms, Inc.). At that point, you are interacting directly with WhatsApp, not with our Site. Any information you share in that WhatsApp conversation — your name, phone number, order details — is governed by WhatsApp’s own Privacy Policy, not ours. We receive your message on WhatsApp just as we would if you had messaged us directly.

We recommend reviewing WhatsApp’s privacy practices at: whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy

3. Third-Party Services Embedded on This Site

3.1 Google Maps

Our Locations page embeds an interactive Google Map so you can find our outlets and get directions. Google Maps is a service provided by Google LLC. When you load the map, Google may:

  • Set cookies on your browser.
  • Collect your IP address and browser information.
  • Track your interactions with the map.

This data is collected and processed by Google according to its own privacy policies. We do not have access to it. You can review Google’s privacy policy at: policies.google.com/privacy

If you prefer not to load Google Maps, simply avoid scrolling to the Locations section of the Site.

3.2 GSAP Animation Library

We load the GSAP (GreenSock Animation Platform) library from a public CDN (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) to power the scroll animations on the Site. This loads static JavaScript files — no personal data is transmitted to GreenSock or Cloudflare in the process.

3.3 Google Fonts

We load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). Google may log your IP address as part of serving these font files. Google Fonts’ privacy practices are covered under Google’s general privacy policy linked above.

4. Cookies

This Site does not set any cookies of its own. However, the embedded Google Map (see Section 3.1) may set cookies on your device. These are third-party cookies controlled by Google, not by Baby Bakers. You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings at any time.

5. Hosting

This Site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. GitHub may log access to the Site for operational purposes (such as IP addresses and request timestamps). GitHub’s privacy practices are available at: docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement

6. Children’s Privacy

This Site is a bakery menu and information website — it is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from children.

7. Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and, where applicable, the GDPR (EU/EEA), you have rights regarding your personal data. However, because this Site does not collect personal data from you directly, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct, or delete. For data collected by third parties (Google through Maps, Meta through WhatsApp), you must exercise your rights directly with those providers.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.
